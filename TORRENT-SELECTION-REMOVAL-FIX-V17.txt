Shigaflow v17 — Torrent selection and removal fix

- Torrent checkboxes are now enabled for every torrent state, including active downloads and metadata retrieval.
- Selecting an active torrent and deleting it detaches it from the torrent engine before removing the list entry.
- Context-menu removal resets bulk-selection mode and refreshes the saved torrent history and summary.
- Choosing Yes in the context menu continues to delete the torrent's content under its selected save folder; choosing No keeps the files.

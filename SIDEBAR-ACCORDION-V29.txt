Shigaflow v29 — sidebar: only one sub-menu open at a time

- Clicking Downloads or Torrents now opens that section's sub-menu and collapses the other one,
  so the two lists never stack up and Settings always stays visible without scrolling.
- Clicking the section you are already on still toggles its own sub-menu open/closed.
- The sidebar's scrollbar is hidden (it is no longer needed at normal window sizes; the mouse wheel
  still works if the window is made unusually short).
- Adding a magnet link switches to the Torrents page without toggling its sub-menu closed.

Files changed: Shigaflow/MainWindow.xaml, Shigaflow/MainWindow.xaml.cs
Not compiled/tested here (no Windows/.NET SDK).

v89 — collapsible sidebar (icon strip, like Windows 11 Task Manager)

- New ☰ button at the top of the sidebar collapses it to a narrow icon strip (64px) and expands it again.
  Ctrl+B does the same. The width slides over ~0.2s. The choice is remembered between launches.
- Collapsed layout:
    * small logo, no app name / tagline
    * Downloads icon, with the open section's category icons stacked underneath, smaller
    * stylised T icon for Torrents (a T whose stem ends in a download arrow), with its category icons underneath
    * Settings icon at the bottom
  Every icon has a tooltip with its name. The +/- markers are hidden.
- The accordion still applies in the strip: clicking the T icon collapses the Downloads category icons
  and opens the Torrents ones (and the other way round). Clicking the icon of the section you are on
  toggles its category icons.
- Icons: Downloads uses the Windows download glyph, Settings the Windows settings glyph, Torrents the new T icon
  (same icons are used in the expanded sidebar).
- Downloads and Torrents tables: the Name column is no longer saved/restored as a fixed pixel width, so it
  stays flexible and the tables fill the extra width when the sidebar collapses (or the window is maximised).
  Other column widths are still saved and restored.

Files changed: Shigaflow/MainWindow.xaml, MainWindow.xaml.cs, AppSettings.cs
Not compiled/tested here (no Windows/.NET SDK).

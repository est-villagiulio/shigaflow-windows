Shigaflow v20 — Torrent details panel + Torrents list alignment

Details panel
- Left-click a torrent row to open a panel docked at the bottom of the Torrents page.
  The list shrinks above it, so you can keep clicking other torrents to switch the panel.
- Close with the X button (top-right of the panel) or the Esc key. Closing also clears the row selection.
- Right-click is unchanged (same context menu) and does not open the panel.
- Clicks on checkboxes, column headers (sort / resize) and scrollbars do not open it.
  While Select mode is active, row clicks do not open it either.
- The panel updates live once per second and closes itself if the torrent is removed.
- Shows: progress bar with status text; Transfer (status, downloaded, download speed, time remaining,
  progress); Sharing (uploaded, upload speed, share ratio, peers, seeds); General (save path,
  total size, added on [HH:mm, dd/MM/yyyy], source, info message).
- Seeds and Uploaded (and therefore Share ratio) are read from the torrent engine only if the
  LibtorrentSharp 0.1.0-alpha build exposes them; otherwise they show "—".

Torrents list
- Row text is aligned with the header text (same fix as the Downloads table).
